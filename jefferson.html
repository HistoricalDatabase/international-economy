PERIOD 4: 1801–1825

The Jeffersonian Era

During the Jeffersonian period, the United States began to view itself not merely as a loose coalition of sovereign states, but as a rising continental power.

Key Milestones of National Development

  • The Louisiana Purchase (1803): Acquiring 828,000 square miles from France doubled the nation's size and secured control of the Mississippi River, giving citizens a shared vision of a continental "Empire of Liberty."
  • The War of 1812: Often called the "Second War of Independence," successfully fighting the British to a stalemate fostered an immense wave of patriotism and proved the republic's permanence.
  • The Era of Good Feelings: Following the conflict, temporary political consensus under President James Monroe minimized partisan strife, solidifying national unity.
