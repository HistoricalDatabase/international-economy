Summary of the Market Revolution

The Market Revolution was a time period where the United States was changed forever from 1815-1846. This period changed the economy by more farming areas, factories, trade, transportation, and inventions. This allowed people to sell products faster. Some transportation methods used were canals, steamboats, railroads, and roads were used to transport products to one place to another in America or to other countries. Some of the famous types of inventions used were the cotton gin and textile machines. Those machines allowed faster and reliable ways of removing, sorting things, creating something faster than any human would. Some other machines were steel plow, interchangeable parts, the sewing machine, telegraph and more. This changed the way humans can create and manufacture goods in a better and faster way. The problem was that the people who worked there had to work for long hours and short breaks. The working environment was very bad too. Everyday life there was just work and earning money. And people's lives changed by being able to afford more clothing and household items. So overall, people at home were shifted into the factory, new classes of workers, and gender roles.
